We love what we do.

A team of engineers, operators and strategists who've made solar our life's work. Based in Auckland, working across New Zealand.

Talk to the team Our services
(01) — Who we areSince day one

Watthaus Strategy was born from a simple belief that the intersection of real estate and renewable energy is where the greatest impact happens.

From residential rooftops in the suburbs to large-scale solar farms in the regions, every project we take on, we treat as our own.

Our process is straightforward: site assessment, tailored design, professional installation and long-term monitoring. We don't cut corners, and we never upsell hardware you don't need.

(02) — Vision

Empowering a sustainable future.

Connecting land, capital and innovation in clean energy to create a renewable future for New Zealand and beyond.

(03) — Mission

Accelerating renewable projects.

Strategic insight, expert collaboration and seamless execution, every single time.

(04) — What drives usOur values

Three values, every project.

The principles we hold ourselves to on every rooftop and every hectare.

01
