Who we are

Watthaus Strategy was born from a simple belief: that the intersection of real estate and renewable energy is where the greatest impact happens.

From residential rooftops in the suburbs to large-scale solar farms in the regions, every project we take on, we treat as our own.

Our process is straightforward: site assessment, tailored design, professional installation and long-term monitoring. We don't cut corners, and we never upsell hardware you don't need.
