(06) — How it worksAssess · Design · Install · Monitor

Four steps, no shortcuts.

Our process is straightforward. We don't cut corners, and we never upsell hardware you don't need.

Step 01 / 04Roof · switchboard · usage

Site assessment.

We look at your roof, switchboard and power usage, in person or from aerial imagery first, so the system fits your home and not a template.

Step 02 / 04Fixed quote

Tailored design.

Every system is modelled for your roof planes, orientation and usage, with clear pricing, honest timelines and realistic ROI before you sign anything.

Step 03 / 04CEC-certified

Professional installation.

A site-specific safety plan, flashed and sealed roof penetrations, careful cable runs and a home that stays fully liveable throughout.

Step 04 / 04Sigen app

Long-term monitoring.

Live data from commissioning day, and annual performance reviews built into the relationship. We stay with you for the life of the system.

New partnership

Powering real estate with renewable energy.

We're delighted to announce a new partnership between Watthaus Strategy Ltd and The WORX Real Estate. By combining WORX's proven expertise in property sales, management and investment with our specialisation in solar and energy strategy, homeowners and landowners can future-proof their assets, strengthen long-term returns and contribute to New Zealand's transition to clean energy.

Together, Watthaus and WORX will identify viable rooftops and properties for solar integration, helping clients enhance property value while reducing their carbon footprint.

  • Unlock new income from underused roof and land space
  • Increase long-term property value through solar
  • Support New Zealand's clean energy transition
  • Create sustainable investments for future generations
