What we deliver

Our services, in detail.

Four stages, one accountable team. Engage us for a single stage or the whole journey.

Identify. Secure. De-risk.

We source, evaluate and secure high-quality land for utility-scale solar.

We manage the full engagement and due diligence process to ensure each project begins with a bankable foundation.

  • Identifying land with optimal solar potential and grid proximity
  • Engaging and negotiating directly with landowners
  • Preparing and securing land term sheets, HOAs and exclusivity agreements
  • Assessing planning, zoning, overlays and environmental constraints
  • Initial site screenings: geotechnical, soil, flood and ecological checks
  • High-level network capacity and feasibility assessments
  • A complete handover package for engineering and design
OutcomeBankable, development-ready land secured with strong landowner relationships and minimal early-stage risk.
Design. Optimise. De-risk.

Robust engineering, from concept design to grid connection.

Our engineering team makes sure every project is feasible, efficient and compliant, with full technical support at every step.

  • Preliminary engineering, site layouts and solar yield modelling
  • Concept, detailed and IFC-level system design
  • DC/AC, MV/HV and SCADA integration design
  • Grid connection studies, submissions and regulatory liaison
  • Constructability analysis and engineering risk assessments
  • QA/QC, design governance and compliance with NZ/AU standards
OutcomeOptimised, compliant and technically validated designs that reduce risk and maximise long-term performance.
Source. Secure. Deliver.

Tier 1 equipment, cost certainty and reliable timelines.

As a Master EPC coordinator, Watthaus Strategy manages the full procurement process from tender to site delivery.

  • Global sourcing of panels, inverters, trackers, transformers and BESS
  • Supplier due diligence, competitive tenders and RFP management
  • Contract negotiation, including warranties, guarantees and service levels
  • International logistics, customs clearance and delivery coordination
  • Supply chain management and just-in-time site delivery
  • Cost optimisation and value engineering to reduce CAPEX
OutcomeHigh-quality equipment at competitive prices, delivered with precision and supply-chain certainty.
Execute. Commission. Energise.

Full construction management for commercial and utility-scale solar.

Safe, efficient, high-quality execution, from civil works through to grid compliance and handover.

  • Site establishment, civil works and access development
  • Mechanical and electrical installation of all solar and BOS systems
  • Subcontractor management and on-site supervision
  • Health & safety management, QA/QC and compliance reporting
  • Testing, commissioning and grid compliance verification
  • Final handover with as-built documentation and O&M onboarding
OutcomePredictable timelines, consistent build quality and smooth energisation into long-term operation.

Real estate × renewables

Land is the first engineering decision. We treat it that way.

Through our network of landowners, real estate partners (including The WORX) and regional councils, we identify suitable sites and run full feasibility on grid proximity, topography, irradiance and consent pathways before recommending anything. We then manage resource consents, grid connection applications and building consents with councils, lines companies and Transpower.
