(07) — Important informationTransparent pricing

Things to know before you book.

“Our goal is to make sure there are no surprises, only clean, affordable energy.” Read through exactly what's included.

01

Offer details
  • Offers apply exclusively to our Bright Start, Bright Boost, Bright Max, Sunny Reserve and Sunny Reserve Plus packages, for residential properties only.
  • All prices are GST-inclusive. Offers cannot be combined with any other promotions or discounts.
  • All installations are subject to Watthaus Strategy's standard Terms of Service.
  • If additional work or costs are identified after the site assessment, a customised quote is provided for your approval before any work begins.
  • Timeframes may vary with product availability, site conditions and local council approvals (where applicable).
02

Roof requirements

Roof pitch

Standard pricing covers roofs with a pitch of up to 25°. Steeper roofs may need tailored mounting and additional safety and labour charges.

Roof type

Prices are based on corrugated iron roofs. Tile, decramastic or membrane roofs may need special mounting systems or extra labour.

Scaffolding

If scaffolding is required, it's charged as an additional cost after the initial site inspection, with a clear breakdown before installation.

03

Switchboard & cabling
  • Standard pricing includes a maximum 5-metre cable run from the switchboard to the inverter or battery.
  • If your switchboard doesn't meet current safety standards, upgrades may be required, especially for whole-home backup in hybrid systems.
  • Extra cabling, trenching or protective conduit runs (if required) are quoted separately.
  • All wiring and components are installed in accordance with New Zealand regulations, for long-term reliability.
