Strategy · Our services

Every stage, one partner.

01
Identify. Secure. De-risk.

Land Acquisition

  • Identify land with optimal solar potential and grid proximity
  • Engage and negotiate directly with landowners
  • Land term sheets, HOAs and exclusivity agreements
  • Planning, zoning, overlay and environmental checks
  • Geotechnical, soil, flood and ecological screening
OutcomeBankable, development-ready land with strong landowner relationships and minimal early-stage risk.
02
Design. Optimise. De-risk.

Engineering

  • Preliminary engineering, site layouts and yield modelling
  • Concept, detailed and IFC-level system design
  • DC/AC, MV/HV and SCADA integration design
  • Grid connection studies, submissions and regulatory liaison
  • QA/QC and compliance with NZ/AU standards
OutcomeOptimised, compliant, technically validated designs that maximise long-term performance.
03
Source. Secure. Deliver.

Procurement

  • Global sourcing of panels, inverters, trackers, transformers and BESS
  • Supplier due diligence, tenders and RFP management
  • Contract negotiation: warranties, guarantees and SLAs
  • International logistics, customs and just-in-time delivery
  • Value engineering to reduce CAPEX
OutcomeTier 1 equipment at competitive prices, delivered with supply-chain certainty.
04
Execute. Commission. Energise.

Construction

  • Site establishment, civil works and access
  • Mechanical and electrical installation of all solar and BOS systems
  • Subcontractor management and on-site supervision
  • Health & safety, QA/QC and compliance reporting
  • Testing, commissioning and O&M onboarding
OutcomePredictable timelines, consistent build quality and smooth energisation.
